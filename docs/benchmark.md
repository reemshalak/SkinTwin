# Benchmark: two leading beauty brand sites vs SkinTwin (30 Oct 2026)

Patterns only. No branding, wording or images from either site are used in SkinTwin.

## What works for them

- **Bold, stacked editorial headlines** in all caps with tight lines, so a page feels like a magazine cover.
- **One big hero and one call to action.** It's always obvious what to do first.
- **Shade swatches on product cards.** You see the colour range before you click.
- **Status badges:** New, Best seller, Limited.
- **A visible mission** (for example, a share of sales funding mental health). It builds trust and emotion.
- **Community photos** from shoppers, and invitations to share your routine.
- **A shade finder quiz** (about 4 steps).
- **A sticky header** with search always available.
- **Product carousels** with "1 of 8"-style pagination.

## What they're missing, and SkinTwin has

- **Results measured by skin scans.** Their reviews are star ratings from strangers.
- **Matching to people with your skin** (skin twins), and verdicts per product *for you*.
- **A verdict for risky products** ("popular but risky for you").
- **Tracking after purchase:** weekly check-ins, goals, keep / pause / swap.
- **Live AR and a skin map.** Neither site shows a virtual try-on.

## What we still lack (backlog)

1. **Editorial typography:** bolder, stacked display headlines on the landing page and key screens.
2. **Swatches on makeup product cards** in Products.
3. **Badges on cards:** "Twin favourite", "Trending", "New in your twins' routines".
4. **A one-line mission/trust statement on the landing page:** "We don't sell anything. Results come from scans, not ratings."
5. **Search in the header**, available everywhere.
6. **More real imagery.** We rely on illustrations; packshots or textures would lift it (MiniMax, optional).

## Done in this pass

- Headlines write themselves in word by word, with a hand-drawn double underline under one key word.
- A soft, skin-toned cursor (mouse only, off for reduced motion).
- A blush wash between pages.
- A Home link in the top bar.
